- Ch2: GameRant "Rebellion of Revolution"; Game8 "Rebellion or Revolution."
- Ch3: GameRant "Greymanes Reunited"; Game8 "Greymanes United."
- Ch4 arc: GameRant/PowerPyx "Mysterious Iron Pot"; Game8 "Mysterious Pot."
- Ch6 arc: GameRant/PowerPyx "Under the Banner Pike Again"; Game8 "Below the Banners."
- Ch6 arc: GameRant/Game8 "The Unyielding Shields"; PowerPyx "The Undying Shields."
- Ch7 arcs: GameRant/PowerPyx "Dawn Mist" and "Dawnrise"; Game8 "Morning Mist" and "Dawn" for the same two arcs.

## Points of no return and world changes
- Chapter 3 → Chapter 4: Damiane becomes temporarily unplayable/unsummonable once the chapter ends, unless her side quests are finished first. Source: vulkk (single source).
- Chapter 7: Ludvig escapes capture and Myurdin retreats with losses rather than being finished off — a permanent story-state change carried into Chapter 8. Source: vulkk (single source).
- Chapter 8: Kliff is knocked unconscious, forcing a Damiane-only playable section that cannot be undone; during it Damiane kills Bastier, who has murdered Beatrice Azerian. Beatrice Azerian's death is permanent. Source: vulkk (single source); Bastier and Beatrice both also named by game8 (588901).
- Chapter 9: no forced order between the four trial questlines (The Calling branches), but Vulkk states a suggested completion order from easiest to hardest: Shattered Ties, Veiled Witch, Thinning Blade, then Six Statues and the Beast. Source: vulkk (single source).
- Chapter 12: after pursuing Caliburn into the Abyss, Damiane and Oongka become unavailable as companions for the rest of the chapter; Vulkk advises removing any critical gear from them first. Source: vulkk (single source).
- Epilogue: completing all 40 Abyss Challenges (a side-content set, not a main quest) unlocks an alternate "true ending" with extra lore about Pywel, the Abyss, and Kliff's backstory. Source: vulkk (single source).
